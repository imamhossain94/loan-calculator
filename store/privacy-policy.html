Privacy Policy — Loan Calculator

Last updated: 21 August 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Loan Calculator Android application (package com.newagedevs.mortgage_calculator, referred to as "the app"), published by NewAgeDevs.

Summary: The app collects no personal data. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no internet access. Everything you type stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal information. Specifically, the app does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, files, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or usage analytics.

Information the app stores on your device

Everything you enter is processed on your device and stays there:

  • Saved calculations. If you tap Save on the Advanced Loan screen, the figures are written to the app's own private storage area. They are readable only by the app.
  • Temporary export files. When you share a result, the app writes a temporary PNG or PDF to its private cache folder so the Android share sheet can pass it on. The cache is cleared each time the app starts.

None of this is transmitted anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. You can also remove it at any time via Android Settings → Apps → Loan Calculator → Storage → Clear data, or by deleting saved entries inside the app.

Permissions

The app requests no Android permissions at all. It does not ask for storage, contacts, location, SMS, call logs, camera, phone state, or an advertising ID.

The app is not granted internet access. It has no network capability and cannot send data anywhere, even in principle.

Sharing, printing and external links

The app has three features that hand information to other apps, and each one happens only when you explicitly ask for it:

  • Share. Choosing Share opens Android's system share sheet and passes the image or PDF to the app you select (for example Gmail, Drive or a messaging app).
  • Print. Choosing Print passes the PDF to Android's print service.
  • Links. Tapping Send feedback, Rate the app or More apps opens your email client, browser or the Google Play app.

Once information is handed to another app, it is governed by that app's privacy policy, not this one. We have no visibility into, or control over, what happens next.

Advertising and analytics

The app contains no advertising SDKs and no analytics SDKs. It does not use the Google Advertising ID. Earlier versions of the app (1.5.6 and older) contained Google AdMob; advertising was removed entirely in version 1.6.0.

Third-party services

The app bundles open-source Flutter packages for on-device functionality such as PDF generation, printing and sharing. None of them collect data or communicate with a server on our behalf.

If you download the app from Google Play, Google may collect information about the download and your device as described in the Google Privacy Policy. That is outside our control and unrelated to the app itself.

Children's privacy

The app is a general-purpose calculator and is not directed at children under 13. Because it collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from children.

Not financial advice

The app performs arithmetic on figures you enter. Results are estimates for information and education only, are not financial advice, and should not be relied on for financial decisions. The app does not offer, broker, arrange, fund or facilitate loans, and is not affiliated with any lender.

Your rights

Data-protection laws such as the GDPR and CCPA grant rights to access, correct, delete or port personal data held about you. As we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, delete, port or disclose. Data stored on your own device is under your control at all times.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date above. If a future version of the app ever begins collecting data, this policy and the app's Google Play Data safety declaration will be updated before that version is released.
